Privacy

Last updated: July 2026.

PreAssess processes the address and project description you submit in order to generate a report: the address is sent to King County and City of Seattle GIS services to resolve the parcel and its regulatory context, and your project description plus retrieved code excerpts are sent to a large-language-model provider to draft the report. Server logs (requests, errors, timing) are kept for operating the service. We do not sell personal information. Reports are not published; addresses you look up are public-record parcel identifiers.

Questions: via the project repository.
